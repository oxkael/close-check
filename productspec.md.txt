Product Spec — CloseWatch 
A market-closure gap signal & calibration dashboard for tokenized US stocks(Working name — rename freely. Built for: Bitget AI × Crypto Hackathon, Genesis Season 2, Track 3 — AI Trading Desk)1. One-linerTokenized US stocks trade 24/7; the real stocks they track do not. CloseWatch measures the price gap that opens up every time the real market is closed (overnight, weekends, holidays), tells the trader how large that gap is right now, and — based on how similar gaps have resolved historically — what typically happens next. The system also tracks its own predictions over time and reports its historical accuracy, and surfaces worst-case outcomes alongside the average case. The trader makes the final call; the system never places trades.2. Problem statementTokenized equities (e.g. rTSLA, rNVDA on Bitget) trade continuously on crypto venues, but their underlying real stock only trades during exchange hours (e.g. NYSE 9:30am–4:00pm ET, weekdays, excluding holidays). During every closure window, the token price can drift away from the last real close — the "gap" — because market makers can't fully hedge until the real market reopens. Traders currently have no quantified way to tell whether a given gap is a real signal or just closure-window noise, or what has historically happened to gaps of that size.3. Target track & subthemesPrimary: Track 3 — AI Trading Desk → "Information insight & signal generation"Secondary (same build, no added scope):"Post-trade review & self-improvement" — via the prediction logging/recalibration loop (Section 8)"Decision stress-testing" — via the tail-risk/worst-case output (Section 7.4)4. GoalsDetect, in real time, whether a tracked ticker's real market is currently closed, and if so, the current gap size vs. last real close.Show historical odds: how often gaps of this size have converged, and how fast, once the real market reopens.Show the historical worst case for gaps of this size, not just the average.Log every flagged gap and its eventual outcome, and periodically recompute the odds from this growing dataset.Present all of this as a dashboard. The system never executes trades or gives direct buy/sell instructions — it surfaces numbers; the human decides.5. Non-goals (explicit — do not build these)No autonomous trade execution of any kind.No coverage of all tokenized tickers — scope is a fixed shortlist (Section 6).No real-money integration, no wallet connection, no order placement.No general news/sentiment analysis — scope is strictly the closure-gap mechanism.No financial-advice language in the UI — frame outputs as historical statistics, not recommendations.6. Core mechanism7.1 Closure windows to trackNightly: every weekday, from real-market close (16:00 ET) to next real-market open (09:30 ET) — confirmed exact boundaries via Bitget's market/states endpoint (Section 9). ~17.5 hours, 5x/week — the highest-frequency window, most data.Weekend: Bitget's formal weekend session — opens Saturday 08:00 or 09:00 UTC+8 (DST-dependent), runs continuously until the real NYSE/NASDAQ open Monday.Market holidays: pull directly from Bitget's market/calendar endpoint (Section 9) — this returns the real holiday/closure list Bitget itself uses, no need to source or hardcode one separately.7.2 Gap definitiongap_pct = (token_price_at_time_T - last_real_close) / last_real_close, computed at multiple sample points during a closure window (e.g. every 30–60 min), plus a final gap reading at the moment the real market reopens.7.3 Calibration (the core statistic)For every historical closure window, bucket the observed gap by size (suggested buckets: <1%, 1–2%, 2–3%, 3–5%, 5–10%, 10–20% — the top bucket is capped near 20% since Bitget enforces a hard ±20% price band on weekend orders, so gaps mechanically can't exceed that) and compute:Convergence rate: % of cases where, after reopen, price moved back toward the pre-closure level (i.e. the gap partially or fully closed) within a defined follow-window (e.g. first 4 trading hours).Average time-to-converge among the cases that did converge.Worst case (tail stat): among cases that did NOT converge, the average/max adverse move against the gap's implied direction. This is the stress-testing output.7.4 Live detection + lookupWhen a ticker's real market is currently closed, compute the live gap, classify it into a bucket, and display that bucket's historical convergence rate, average time-to-converge, and worst-case tail stat.8. Self-improvement loop (post-trade review subtheme)Every time the live detector flags a gap, log: ticker, timestamp, bucket, gap size. Once the closure window ends and the real market has been open for the defined follow-window, record the actual outcome (converged / did not converge, time taken, final move size). On a regular cadence (e.g. daily), recompute the bucket statistics from Section 7.3 including this new logged data. The dashboard should show a visible, dated "current model accuracy" or "last N predictions" summary — this is the demo-ready proof that the system reviews and improves itself, not just a static backtest.9. System architectureCodeData Ingestion: scheduled job pulling token prices (frequent) and real close prices (once per market close).Closure Detector: given a ticker and timestamp, returns whether the real market is currently closed (checks exchange hours + holiday calendar).Gap Calculator: computes gap_pct at each sample point.Calibration Engine: maintains the bucketed historical stats (Section 7.3) and updates them via the self-improvement loop (Section 8).Prediction Log: append-only record of every flagged gap and its eventual outcome.FastAPI service: exposes endpoints (Section 11) to the dashboard.Dashboard: displays per-ticker current status (Section 12).11. Suggested data schema (tables)tickers (ticker, underlying_name, exchange, added_at)token_prices (ticker, timestamp, price)real_closes (ticker, date, close_price)closure_windows (ticker, type[nightly|weekend|holiday], start_ts, end_ts, last_close_price, reopen_price)gap_observations (ticker, closure_window_id, timestamp, gap_pct)calibration_buckets (bucket_range, ticker_scope[per-ticker or all], convergence_rate, avg_time_to_converge, worst_case_move, sample_size, last_updated)prediction_log (id, ticker, flagged_at, bucket, gap_pct_at_flag, outcome[pending|converged|not_converged], resolved_at, actual_move_pct)12. Build timeline (9 days)Days 1–2: Data pipeline — pull token + real close history for the 5–8 chosen tickers; align on closure windows.Days 2–3: Gap calculation across historical closure windows.Days 3–4: Calibration engine — bucket stats, convergence rate, time-to-converge, worst-case tail.Days 5–6: Live closure detector + live gap calculation.Day 6–7: Wire live gap → bucket lookup → prediction logging.Day 7–8: Dashboard (per-ticker cards + summary/accuracy screen).Day 8–9: Polish, record a demo walkthrough using a real historical case, finalize the accuracy summary number for the pitch.15. Edge cases / failure modes to handleNew or thinly-traded ticker: too few historical closures for a meaningful bucket sample — display "insufficient data" rather than a fabricated stat.Exchange holiday calendar must be correct — a wrong "market closed" check invalidates every downstream number.API rate limits on free tiers — cache aggressively, don't poll live price more often than needed.Token trading pauses/halts on the crypto side — distinguish "no token data" from "gap is zero."16. Success criteria for the demoLive per-ticker gap + odds display, working for at least the top 2–3 tickers.At least one real historical case shown end-to-end (gap detected → historical odds shown → what actually happened).A visible, non-zero "predictions logged / accuracy so far" number — even a small sample is more credible than a claim with no logged track record.Clear on-screen language that this is decision support, not financial advice, and not an execution system.17. GlossaryGap: the % difference between current token price and the last official close of the real underlying stock, measured while the real market is closed.Convergence: the real market price, once reopened, moving back toward where the token had drifted during the closure.Bucket: a size-range grouping of gaps (e.g. 1–2%) used to compute historical statistics.Closure window: any period the real stock market is closed but the token continues trading (nightly, weekend, or holiday).
